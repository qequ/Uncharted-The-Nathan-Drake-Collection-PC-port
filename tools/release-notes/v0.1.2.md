**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port by **BilalReloaded**
([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BilalReloaded/videos), [GitHub](https://github.com/bc160201983)).

On 0.1.1? The launcher offers this update: press **UPDATE TO 0.1.2** next to PLAY.

## Fixed

- **Uncharted 3:** crashes in the opening bar fight and the Cartagena museum, and characters flying
  around.
- **Uncharted 1:** crash after the opening boat scene.
- **Uncharted 2:** NPCs coming from the sky, NPCs floating in the air, Nate's feet sinking into the
  floor and NPC legs flickering while they walk. Every preset queues **1 frame** again; the update
  moves preset settings back from 2 to 1 for you.
- **Uncharted 2:** blurry background while aiming. The aiming depth of field never got the distance
  to the crosshair (it is computed on the GPU), and above 1080p it measured the wrong spot of the
  screen. Sharp at every resolution now.
- **Uncharted 2:** GPU hang ("Device lost") after a long session, caused by the treasure pickup
  effect.
- **Multithreaded rendering:** the crashes within minutes (Uncharted 3) and the floating NPCs
  (Uncharted 2) are fixed. It is still experimental and gives no speed-up on fast CPUs.

## Faster

- About **12% more FPS in the heaviest Uncharted 2 scenes** (and about 9% less CPU work per frame in
  Uncharted 3): optimizations that were switched off while bugs were hunted are on again.
- **74-85% less data copied back from the GPU** every second in all three games (Uncharted 2: about
  560 -> 90 MB/s): copies the game never reads are skipped. Helps slower PCs most.
- Less CPU work per draw (about 20% in heavy Uncharted 2 scenes).

## New

- More frame rate limits in the in-game menu and the launcher: **40, 45, 50 and 55 FPS** (also handy
  to compare in tests).

## Known issues

- **Uncharted 3:** Sully's shirt in the bar can flicker at 60 FPS (not at 30). Still being looked at.
- The heaviest Uncharted 2 scenes run at about 50 FPS (the PS4's GPU command processing runs on one
  CPU core). More optimization is planned.
- **Recording with OBS Game Capture:** if OBS keeps losing and re-hooking the game, the game's memory
  use grows. With an NVIDIA card set NVIDIA Control Panel -> Manage 3D settings -> Program settings ->
  Uncharted.exe -> **Vulkan/OpenGL present method: Prefer native**, or use OBS **Window Capture**.
- **Frames queued 2** is faster in heavy scenes, but GPU results reach the game a frame later:
  keep it at 1.
- Report problems with the log from the launcher's **Advanced -> Open log folder**.

## Install (new players)

1. Download **Uncharted-windows.zip** below and extract it anywhere.
2. Start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and select your game folder (the dumped `CUSA02344` folder with
   `eboot.bin` and `sce_sys`). Recommended: update 1.02 next to it, named like it plus `-patch`.
3. Pick a game and a preset and press **PLAY**.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own dump of the Collection (CUSA02344). Not
> affiliated with Sony, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint Games.
